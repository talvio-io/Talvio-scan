TALVIO SCAN — DEPLOY

Owner-only page. Deploy the Talvio Scan system on BNB Chain: token ($SCAN), 8-hour-round scan NFT, and the claim contract — then fund the claim pool with exactly 6B $SCAN. Nothing here deploys or moves funds without your wallet confirmation. 8-hour rounds, 3 per day: each scan mints 1 NFT, each NFT claims $SCAN after its round's claims open.

$Cash math

  • 10 paid scans/round = 0.02 BNB/round straight to the project wallet (~$11/round at ~$550/BNB, up to 3 rounds/day).
  • Paid scan 0.002 BNB each; claim fee 0.000277 BNB (Royal 0.000555 BNB) — 100% to the project wallet in the same transaction. Nothing is kept in any contract.
  • Free scan: 1 per wallet per round, from a shared quota of 500 per 8-hour round (00/08/16 UTC).
