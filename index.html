THE RHYTHM

Talvio Scan runs on a fixed UTC clock — three 8-hour rounds a day, each sealing as it ends, claims opening one round later. Same rhythm, every day. Set your watch by it.

00 · 08
16 UTC

A new round opens

Three 8-hour rounds a day: 00:00–08:00, 08:00–16:00 and 16:00–24:00 UTC. Each round carries 500 free scans — one free per wallet — first come first served. When the free quota runs out, paid scans stay open.

8 HOURS

Scanning runs

Free scan once per wallet per round, paid scans at 0.002 BNB — up to 10 scans per wallet per round. Every scan counts toward that round's unique-agent total, and that total sets everyone's tier.

ROUND END

The round seals

The moment a round ends — 08:00, 16:00 or 24:00 UTC — it seals: the unique-agent count locks in and every Orb's claim amount is final.

+8 HOURS

Claims open — forever

Eight hours after a round ends — at the end of the next round — claims open for that round's Orbs. Scan in the 00:00 round and you claim from 16:00 the same day. No deadline after that: claims never expire.

11

QUESTIONS

Short answers. Exact numbers. No fine print.

How many free scans do I get?+
One free scan per wallet per round. The free pool is shared: 500 free scans per 8-hour round (00–08 · 08–16 · 16–24 UTC), first come first served. If your round runs out, the next one opens at the next 8-hour mark — or scan paid.
Do I need a Binance Agent ID?+
Yes — every scan, free or paid, requires a Binance Agent ID held by the scanning wallet. The contract checks the Agent ID registry on-chain; without one, the scan reverts.
How many scans can I do in a round?+
Up to 10 scans per wallet per round — your 1 free scan plus up to 9 paid ones. The limit resets every 8 hours, when the next round starts.
What does a paid scan cost?+
0.002 BNB per paid scan, plus normal network gas. The fee goes straight to the project wallet — nothing sits in the contracts.
What does claiming cost?+
0.000277 BNB per Orb. Royal Orbs pay 0.000555 BNB. That's the whole cost — the $SCAN amount itself is paid out of the 6B claim pool.
What are my chances of a Royal Orb?+
About 3% of scans mint a Royal Orb, decided on-chain at the moment of the scan. A Royal pays 250,000 $SCAN regardless of how busy its round was.
Do claims expire?+
Never. A round seals the moment it ends, and claims open 8 hours later. From then on that Orb can be claimed at any time, for the same amount, forever.
Where do the fees go?+
Scan and claim fees go straight to the project wallet with each transaction. The contracts hold only the $SCAN claim pool — no BNB accumulates in them.
